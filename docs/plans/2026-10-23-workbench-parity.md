### Decision Gate

Recommended research direction: a pinned MIT magpie sidecar with its full web UI, supervised through a small adapter, while OFM retains its own channels and public gateway contract. This can preserve most M01-M43 backend/UI behavior without recreating it. It does **not** automatically supply Codex++ CDP enhancements, CC Switch-specific client/profile semantics, native GUI-only features, or the independent Codex Desktop package-context launch requirement.

Before adopting it, require a bounded proof covering: dedicated data/ports without touching native profiles; all removed products denied including dynamic plugins; correct admin cookie and nested plugin pages in the chosen host; OFM-to-engine streaming/tool and model-ID fidelity without loops; one refresh owner; startup/readiness/quit/crash/restart behavior; signed/pinned runtime/resource packaging. Use fixtures first, then the V01-V24 native/live prerequisites. This audit only establishes source feasibility and blockers; it implements, builds and starts none of this.
